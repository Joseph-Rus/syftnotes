Support

Something not right? Tell us.

Syft Notes is made in the open, on GitHub. That's where to ask a question, report a problem or suggest something, and where you can see what's being worked on. If you'd rather write to us, email support@syftnotes.com.

Email us support@syftnotes.com: tell us what you did, what happened, and your version. Report a problem Open an issue: what you did, what happened, and what you expected. Ask or suggest Search the issues first: someone may have asked already. A security problem Report it privately, not in a public issue. You'll get a reply within a week.

What helps

  • Your version, from Syft Notes' Settings, and whether it's a Mac or Windows.
  • One computer or two, and which one the problem is on: the laptop or the library.
  • What you saw: the exact words of a message, or a screenshot.
  • Never your notes, recordings, passwords or codes. Issues are public. Blur anything personal in a screenshot.

Worth trying first

  • Update. In Settings, click Update now: under Your library → Library, or on a laptop under General → Updates for the laptop itself. Only the newest release gets fixes.
  • Can't open it on a Mac? It needs macOS 15 or later. Copies from before 0.11 weren't signed: download the newest one, or go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  • "Windows protected your PC"? Click More info, then Run anyway.
  • The laptop can't find the library? Check the library's computer is on, awake and running Syft Notes, and, away from home, that Tailscale is connected on both.
  • Phone app trouble? The phone guide covers the usual ones.
  • Notes not being written? Check the engine in Settings → Your library → Your AI: it says when one isn't installed, signed in or running.
