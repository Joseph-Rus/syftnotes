  • Check your notes. Transcripts and notes are written by software and AI, which make mistakes. Check them against the lecture before relying on them for an exam or an assignment.
  • Keep your own backups. Your library lives on your own computers, so backing it up is up to you. Your notes are plain files in ~/.study-stash, easy to copy.

Recording

Record only where you're allowed to. Many schools, instructors and places require everyone's consent before a class or conversation is recorded, and some don't allow it at all. Following your school's rules and the law where you are is your responsibility.

Other services

Study Stash can work with services you choose and sign in to yourself: Canvas, Google Calendar, Microsoft calendars, Tailscale, and AI engines such as Ollama, Claude Code, Codex and Gemini. Your use of each is under that service's own terms, and of your school's for Canvas. Study Stash only reads from Canvas and your calendars. The privacy policy says what it reads and where it goes.
