## What's new

* **Setup asks what you have.** The first screen has three answers in plain words: **I have a paid Claude plan**, **I have a paid ChatGPT plan** and **I don't have a paid plan**. Before, it asked you to pick an AI by the tools underneath (Claude Code, Codex), and left a student with no plan a small grey line.
* **No plan? Study Stash still works.** Without an AI it records and writes down every lecture, and you can add one later in Settings → Your AI. For a free one there's **Free AI on this Mac** (or PC), marked Advanced: **Set it up** downloads Ollama from its maker, installs it, starts it and downloads a model your computer can run, showing how far along it is. It picks the model by your computer's memory, says its size before anything downloads, and says so first if the disk has no room. Before, you were sent to ollama.com to do each step yourself.
* **Claude and ChatGPT with a button, in setup by hand too.** **Set it up** on their rows opens the maker's own installer and then its sign-in page, and comes back with that AI picked. Before, the rows gave commands to paste into Terminal.
* **Your AI.** Settings' AI engines page is now **Your AI**, and an AI goes by the name you know everywhere: Claude and ChatGPT, where Study Stash said Claude Code and Codex. A lecture says "Written by Claude". Settings' AI tool access is now **AI apps**.
* **Ten more colour themes.** Sky, Midnight, Violet, Lilac, Bubblegum, Peach, Mocha, Moss, Clover and Mint: twenty in all, in Settings → Appearance and in the phone app's Settings.

## Fixes

All of these are on Windows.

* **The record shortcut leaves your keyboard alone.** Pressing Ctrl+Alt+R brought the small recorder up as the window in front, so what you typed next went to it and not to Word or your browser. It now shows without taking the keyboard; click it to open it and ask a question.
* **A minimized Study Stash comes back.** With its window minimized, Open Study Stash, opening the app again and clicking a lecture in the tray only lit its taskbar button. The window now comes back up, as large as it was.
* **No waiting for an AI you don't have.** Opening a page under Settings → Your library, and Continue on setup's notes step, waited four or five seconds when Ollama wasn't installed. It's about a second now.
* **A plainer tray panel.** Record and Search no longer show their shortcuts beside them, and the line at the bottom ("Library running on this PC · Model ready") is shown whole, where it was cut short.
* **Windows' own words.** An empty Home says to record "from the tray" (it said the menu bar), a diagram's window says Ctrl + scroll to zoom (it said ⌘/Ctrl), and Settings names the terminal a class opens in as Windows Terminal (it said ghostty). Setup's first screen no longer has a scroll bar.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → Your library → Library → Update now (a laptop: Settings → General → Updates), or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
