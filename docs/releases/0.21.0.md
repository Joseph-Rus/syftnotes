## New

* **Study.** A new Study page in the sidebar, with how many review cards are due, for getting ready for exams:
  * **Review:** Make review cards on a lecture's page has your AI write a dozen flashcards from its notes. Study brings each one back just before you'd forget it: Space shows the answer, then 1 to 4 for Again, Hard, Good or Easy, spaced the way Anki spaces them.
  * **Exam prep:** Study lists the exams coming up in Canvas and your calendar. Prep for it writes a study guide and a practice test from the class's lectures since the last exam, each point with the lecture and moment it comes from. The questions you miss go into Review.
* **What's on the exam.** A lecture's page lists every time the lecturer said something will be on the exam ("this will be on the midterm", "make sure you know", "write this down"), each with its moment in the recording. It's found on your computer, without AI.
* **Listen.** Listen on a lecture's page turns it into a five-minute conversation between a tutor and a classmate, read aloud by your computer's own voices, to listen to on a walk.
* **Recipes in Ask.** Type / in the Ask bar for ready-made questions (a study guide, what's likely on the exam, key terms, practice questions and more), or keep your own with the bookmark button.
* **Syft Notes for Linux.** x64 and ARM64 tarballs, recording from PipeWire or PulseAudio, with the tray and global shortcuts on X11.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11; on Linux, a desktop with PipeWire or PulseAudio. Listen on Linux needs espeak-ng.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → General → Updates → Update now.
* **The phone app** can use recipes in Ask; Review, exam prep and Listen are on the computer for now.
