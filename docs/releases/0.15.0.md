## What's new

* **Search Settings.** A search field now sits at the top of Settings. Type what you're after, like "dark", "zoom" or "keep recordings", and it lists the settings that match and the section each is in. Choose one, and Settings opens that page and lights the setting up. Return opens the first match, Escape clears the search, and ⌘F (Ctrl+F on Windows) jumps to the field.
* **Ask your AI in Settings.** The new **Ask your AI** page, at the top of the sidebar, lets you tell your own Claude or ChatGPT what you want ("make it dark", "record my Zoom lectures too", "keep recordings for a week"). It finds the setting, shows it to you, and changes it. You see each change happen in Settings, and you can change it back there. It can switch dark mode, the colour theme, start at login, updates, the shortcuts, the transcription model, when a lecture is written down, its language, recording the computer's sound, telling speakers apart, how long recordings are kept, your name, writing notes, sorting and how sure sorting must be, and it can add classes. For anything else, like passwords, Canvas, AI apps or what this computer is for, it opens the page and tells you what to press. It runs the same way as guided setup's chat: the Claude Code or Codex you signed in to there, limited to Egghead Notes' own settings, and it never sees your passwords. If nothing in the search matches, choose **Ask your AI about "…"** under the results.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11. Ask your AI needs Claude Code or Codex on this computer. Setup can install it and sign you in.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → Your library → Library → Update now (a laptop: Settings → General → Updates), or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
