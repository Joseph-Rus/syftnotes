## What's new

* **Due has a home of its own.** Due is back in the sidebar: what's to hand in (overdue, this week, next week, later) and by class, with the ask bar, and Full list for the two-column list. Home's See all and To hand in open it.
* **Record and ask from Home and a class's home.** A full-size red Record button records into that class, or for the library to sort from Home. The ask bar at the foot of the page asks about everything, or just that class.
* **Class folders.** Classes can sit in folders of your own in the sidebar. A class's context menu moves it to Classes, another folder, or a new one. A class can also link folders on your computer (a repo, say): they show on the class's home, and the AI searches and reads them.
* **A tidier class home.** A class opens on its home, with one way into each place: Recent lectures' See all (and the lecture count) for its lectures, and cards for Canvas's lists. Lectures are one list with the Canvas switcher above it.
* **The list folds away.** The list column folds away from its own header like the sidebar (⌃⌘L on a Mac, Ctrl+Shift+L on Windows) and comes back from the page's edge, and it's remembered. The title bar has one toggle, for the sidebar. Notes, assignments and Canvas pages sit centred in the room they have.
* **A new recorder pill.** A small dark upright capsule: Study Stash's mark on top, three green bars moving with the microphone, Stop under the pointer, a pause mark while paused, and the time as its tooltip.
* **API keys for Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini** in Settings, each in a key box of its own with a short line saying what it's for.
* **Update now from the menu bar** on a Mac.
* **Fixes.** A Mac toast's × is on the top-left. On Windows, a long class name in the list's header wraps and ends in … instead of being clipped.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording. To update now, use Settings → Your library → Library → Update now (a laptop: Settings → General → Updates), or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
