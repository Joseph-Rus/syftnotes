## What's new

* **Notes and Transcript stay at the top of a note.** Scroll down a long note or transcript and its Notes | Transcript tabs stay pinned at the top of the page (while it records, with the time beside them), so switching between your notes and the live transcript never means scrolling all the way back up. Switching from there shows the other tab from its start.

## Fixes

* **A quieter level meter.** The bars beside the time while you record are three, not five.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → Your library → Library → Update now (a laptop: Settings → General → Updates), or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
