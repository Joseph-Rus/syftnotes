## What's new

* **Setup picks the speech model for you.** There's no model step any more: Study Stash picks one that keeps up on your computer and stays light (on most computers the compact Whisper model, 574 MB, about 0.8 GB of memory while you record), and downloads it while you finish setting up. Nothing changes if you already have a model.
* **Remove a model you downloaded.** In Settings → Recording, each downloaded model you aren't using has its own Remove, to free the space. It asks first, and you can download it again any time. Cactus Whistle comes with the app, so there's nothing to remove.

## Fixes

* **A refused API key says so.** Settings → Your AI saved a key the AI company turned away as if it were fine, and never showed any of its messages. It now says what came of each key and each change, where you made it.
* **Your AI keeps working after the library's password changes.** With Settings open, turning on laptops or changing the password made Your AI and AI apps say the library wasn't answering until Settings was reopened.
* **Log out ends the sign-in.** Signing out of your library's own web pages now ends that sign-in on the library itself. A new library password signs out every browser that used the old one, and guessing the password is slowed and then refused for a while.
* **Hide Others is ⌥⌘H on a Mac.** It was on ⌥⌘Q, one key from Quit.

## Good to know

* **Browsers are signed out once.** If you use your library's web pages in a browser, you'll be asked for the library password once after this update. Laptops, paired phones, the Canvas extension and AI apps aren't affected.
* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → Your library → Library → Update now (a laptop: Settings → General → Updates), or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
