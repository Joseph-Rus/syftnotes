## What's new

* **A new look.** Study Stash is redrawn: black, grey and white, in a typeface made to be easy to read, with each class's highlighter the one colour and red only for recording and for what's late.
* **One sidebar, one page.** The sidebar has Home, Due, then your folders under Classes, Clubs and Work, and what's coming up. A folder has one page: its notes by week, and beside them what's due, its lists on Canvas and when it next meets. A note fills the page, and you rename it by typing over its title.
* **Home shows what's next.** The date, a sentence on what's pressing, **Up next** (click an event to record it) and every note, newest first. Notes in no folder are here too, marked Unsorted: the folder under a note's title moves it, with Undo.
* **Type your own notes while it records.** Recording opens the lecture's page at once. Type there as it records, as much or as little as you like. The write-up keeps every line of yours, in ink, and adds what the lecture said in grey. **Only my notes** hides what was added.
* **Due is a calendar.** By month or by week, each thing due in its class's highlighter, with Missing under what's late and a line through what's handed in.
* **Bring your lectures over from Granola.** If you also record in Granola, open **Settings → Granola** and press **Connect**. You sign in on Granola's own page in your browser, and Study Stash never sees your password. From then on it checks every few minutes for lectures it doesn't have. A new one gets a notification with a **Transfer** button, and the Granola page lists every one that's waiting, each with **Transfer** and **Leave it**. A transferred lecture is filed and gets study notes, like one you recorded here. Granola's free plan shares each lecture's notes from the last 30 days, and the study notes are written from Granola's summary. A paid Granola plan shares the transcript too, and the notes are written from that.

## Fixes

* **Saving Settings keeps a class added meanwhile.** A class added from somewhere else while Settings was open (by your AI, or from another computer) was dropped the next time Settings saved. It's kept now, and saving the library's page also leaves each class in its sidebar folder.
* **No flash of magenta.** The whole window could turn magenta for a moment when a page with the Ask bar opened.

## Good to know

* **What moved:** Unsorted is no longer in the sidebar (those notes are on Home), and a class's tabs and tiles are gone: everything a class had is on its one page. A recording is called a lecture in a class, a meeting in a club or work folder, and a recording when it's in no folder.
* **Granola is new.** If a lecture doesn't come over the way it should, [tell us](https://github.com/Joseph-Rus/study-stash/issues). Settings → Granola is on the computer you record on. Study Stash only reads from Granola and changes nothing there, and **Disconnect** forgets the sign-in. Study Stash isn't affiliated with Granola.
* **Not changed yet:** the phone app and the layouts of Settings and setup keep their old arrangement, in the new colours and type.
* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → Your library → Library → Update now (a laptop: Settings → General → Updates), or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
