## What's new

* **Bring your lectures over from Granola.** If you also record in Granola, open **Settings → Granola** and press **Connect**. You sign in on Granola's own page in your browser, and Study Stash never sees your password. From then on it checks every few minutes for lectures it doesn't have. A new one gets a notification with a **Transfer** button, and the Granola page lists every one that's waiting, each with **Transfer** and **Leave it**. A transferred lecture is filed under its class and gets study notes, like one you recorded here.
* **What comes over depends on your Granola plan.** Granola's free plan shares each lecture's notes from the last 30 days, and Study Stash writes its study notes from Granola's summary. A paid Granola plan shares the transcript too, and the notes are written from that.

## Fixes

* **Saving Settings keeps a class added meanwhile.** A class added from somewhere else while Settings was open (by your AI, or from another computer) was dropped the next time Settings saved. It's kept now, and saving the library's page also leaves each class in its sidebar folder.

## Good to know

* **It's new.** If a lecture doesn't come over the way it should, [tell us](https://github.com/Joseph-Rus/study-stash/issues).
* **Where it is:** Settings → Granola is on the computer you record on. Study Stash only reads from Granola and changes nothing there, and **Disconnect** forgets the sign-in. Study Stash isn't affiliated with Granola.
* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → Your library → Library → Update now (a laptop: Settings → General → Updates), or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
