## What's new

* **Bring your lectures over from Granola.** If you also record in Granola, open **Settings → Granola** and press **Connect**. You sign in on Granola's own page in your browser, and Egghead Notes never sees your password. From then on it checks every few minutes for lectures it doesn't have. A new one gets a notification with a **Transfer** button, and the Granola page lists every one that's waiting, each with **Transfer** and **Leave it**. A transferred lecture is filed and gets study notes, like one you recorded here.
* **What comes over depends on your Granola plan.** Granola's free plan shares each lecture's notes from the last 30 days, and Egghead Notes writes its study notes from Granola's summary. A paid Granola plan shares the transcript too, and the notes are written from that.

## Fixes

* **A note's length is shown whole.** On Home and on a folder's page, a lecture over an hour showed its length cut short ("1 h 12 mi").

## Good to know

* **Granola is new.** If a lecture doesn't come over the way it should, [tell us](https://github.com/Joseph-Rus/study-stash/issues). Settings → Granola is on the computer you record on. Egghead Notes only reads from Granola and changes nothing there, and **Disconnect** forgets the sign-in. Egghead Notes isn't affiliated with Granola.
* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → Your library → Library → Update now (a laptop: Settings → General → Updates), or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
