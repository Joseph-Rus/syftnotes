## Fixes

* **No more crashes while a long lecture records.** With speakers turned on, a lecturer who talked for more than about two minutes without a break made Egghead Notes quit about twelve minutes in, and again each time it picked the recording up. Your recording was always kept; now it just keeps going.
* **The recorder pill's level meter moves with the voice.** Its bars were scaled for a microphone right in front of you, so someone talking across the room barely moved them. They now follow how loud the last few seconds were.
* **The pill's card stays put while you move the pointer on it.** On a Mac, moving the pointer over the small pill made Stop and the card beside it flicker; they only settled when the pointer stopped.
* **You can click right next to the pill.** On a Mac, a clear box around the pill, wider than the pill itself, caught clicks meant for whatever was behind it.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → Your library → Library → Update now (a laptop: Settings → General → Updates), or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
