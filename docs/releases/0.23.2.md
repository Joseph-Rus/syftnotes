## Fixes

* **After an update, Syft Notes comes back the way you left it.** If its window was open when an update installs, the window opens again (before, the app came back only in the menu bar or the tray). This starts with the update after this one: the update to 0.23.2 itself still comes back in the menu bar or the tray.
* **The AI you sign in to writes your notes.** Sign in to Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, and that AI writes your notes and answers your questions from then on. You don't need to change it in Settings. Signing in again to an AI you already use keeps your choices as they are.
* **Update now works right after a new version comes out.** For up to an hour after a release, Update now could say "Syft Notes is up to date" and do nothing. It now asks for the newest version each time you press it.
* **On Windows, the old copy is cleared away.** Moving to the new way of updating (0.23.0) left the old copy behind, so Syft Notes was listed twice in Settings → Apps, and the app could be closed for several minutes during the move. Syft Notes now removes the old copy by itself, and clicking a Syft Notes notification opens the right copy again. Your lectures and settings aren't touched.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11; on Linux, a desktop with PipeWire or PulseAudio.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within about an hour, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → General → Updates → Update now.
