## Changes

* **Add a lecture from a recording you already have.** A voice memo, a Zoom or Teams recording, a podcast, or a video of the lecture (.m4a, .mp3, .wav, .mp4, .mov and more) becomes a lecture like any you record: written down on your computer, then your AI writes the notes, and you can ask about it and study it. Press **Add a recording** beside Record (or use Record's small menu), or drop the file on Home or a class's page. Pick the class (or let the library sort it) and the day it happened. For a video only the sound is used, and your file is never moved or changed.
* **A clearer Study page.** Review is one card: how many cards are due, Start, and each class's cards as a small pill (click one to review just that class). Your exams are one list, each on its date with its class, how soon it is and your last practice-test score, and one button: Prep for it or Open prep. Another exam is one row: pick the class from a menu, type the exam's name, Write the prep.
* **Dark mode looks as good as light.** Cards stand out from the page, the sidebar is a step darker, edges show, and class colours are the same bright highlighters as in light, with dark words on them.

## Fixes

* **Buttons, cards and fields have clean, whole edges.** Some lost an edge or a corner (the class buttons on Study did).
* **Text fields match the rest of the app,** with the same rounded corners and colours, in light and dark.
* **A button you can't press yet looks it,** a quiet grey instead of a faded copy of the main button.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11; on Linux, a desktop with PipeWire or PulseAudio.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within about an hour, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → General → Updates → Update now.
* **A long video takes a while** to be written down, and a lecture you record meanwhile waits until it's done.

* **Windows:** this update downloads in full (about 140 MB) rather than as a small update.
