## Changes

* **Share a lecture with a friend.** On a lecture's page, press Share and pick **Send to a Syft Notes user…**. Syft Notes saves the lecture as one `.syft` file with its notes and its transcript. Send it any way you like: AirDrop, email, a chat or a USB stick. The recording and any attachments stay with you.
* **Open a lecture a friend shared.** Double-click the `.syft` file on a Mac or a Windows PC, drop it on Home or a class's page, or pick it with **Add a recording**. Syft Notes asks one thing: which class it goes in. It starts on your class with the same name, or you can let the library sort it. Then it's a lecture like your own, marked "Shared by a friend": search, Ask and Study all work on it, and your AI can rewrite its notes. Opening the same file again never makes a copy.
* **One Add a recording button.** Record no longer has its own small menu that did the same thing as **Add a recording** beside it. Press **Add a recording** to bring in a recording you already have, or drop the file on Home or a class's page.
* **Windows: signed by its publisher.** Setup.exe and the app are now signed, so Windows shows who made them.

## Fixes

* **Mac: dropdowns and menus open on the desktop you're on.** With more than one desktop, a dropdown or menu could open on another one and seem not to open at all.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11; on Linux, a desktop with PipeWire or PulseAudio.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within about an hour, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → General → Updates → Update now.
* **A `.syft` file is the lecture itself,** so anyone who has the file can read it: its title, class, day, notes (with the lines you typed into them) and transcript. It holds no names, no recording and nothing else from your computer.
