## What's new

* **Chat history in Ask.** Your conversations are kept. A lecture's page opens on its latest chat, so you can pick up where you left off, even after closing Egghead Notes. Chats, at the top of the Ask card and in the ask bar, lists your past conversations: choose one to carry it on. Follow-ups such as "and the second one?" or "explain that more simply" now know what was just said. New chat starts a fresh one, and a chat can be renamed, or deleted with Undo. A folder's page lists its own chats and its lectures', and Home lists them all. Chats are kept on your library, so your laptop sees them too.
* **Undo and History for your notes.** Every change to a lecture's notes is kept: notes your AI wrote, a rewrite, your own edits, notes pasted in. Right after a change, Undo appears over the notes (⌘Z, or Ctrl+Z on Windows). History, in the notes' header, lists every version ("Written by Claude", "Your edit"), shows what each would change, and puts any of them back.
* **No paid AI? You can still get notes.** Ask the free Claude app to "write notes for this lecture" and they appear in Egghead Notes. You can switch this off in Settings → AI apps ("Save notes they write"). Or use any free chat on the web: on a lecture with no notes, press Copy for your AI, paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, then bring back what it writes with Paste notes. Your own typed lines are always kept, and notes are never replaced without asking.
* **Type your notes in Markdown.** While a lecture records, the notes you type turn into headings (#), bullets (-), numbered lists (1.), checkboxes ([ ]) and quotes (>) as you type, and **bold**, *italic*, `code`, links and maths show formatted. ⌘B and ⌘I (Ctrl+B and Ctrl+I on Windows) make words bold or italic. Your formatting is kept in the notes your AI writes around them.
* **Select and copy almost anything.** Drag to select text in transcripts (each line keeps its time), in chats, the recorder's live words, assignments, Canvas pages and announcements, and in error messages, addresses and codes, so you can paste them to get help. Several lines pasted into Ask now go in whole.
* **Set up with any AI.** Gemini is on setup's first screen beside Claude and ChatGPT. Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini can each use an API key on the notes step. With no paid plan, the free AI on your computer uses a model that's already installed instead of asking for a big download. Setup also tries your AI once before saving it, so a signed-out one is caught right there.
* **A Gemini key made today works.** New keys use Gemini 3.8 Flash, and Flash-Lite once the free daily limit is used up. Google's free tier may use what you send to improve its products; the free AI on your computer keeps lectures private.
* **Simpler setup for one computer.** Setup asks "Where your lectures are kept", with Just this Mac (or PC) chosen. A laptop and a computer at home are under Advanced.

## Fixes

* When Egghead Notes asks your Claude or ChatGPT to write notes or answer a question, it no longer brings in your own add-ons (connected apps and servers) or your own Claude Code hooks.
* Typing "- " or "# " in your notes while recording no longer leaves "- - " behind.
* Setup's chat no longer shows a question twice, ChatGPT's replies no longer show raw ** marks, and the download percent on the last card keeps moving.
* Spinners stay smooth however busy the app is.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Your library:** chat history and the new notes features need your library to be on 0.17.2 too. If your library is another computer, it updates itself.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → General → Updates → Update now, or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
