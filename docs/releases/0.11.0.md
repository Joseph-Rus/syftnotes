## What's new

* **Diagrams you can explore.** Point at a box and it lights up with its arrows; click it to pin it and ask Explain this, Quiz me or Where was this said? (which opens the transcript at that moment). Step through a diagram a box at a time, Test yourself hides its words for you to recall, a big chart opens as an overview you unfold a group at a time, and ⌘ or Ctrl with the wheel zooms. On paper it's the same still picture as before.
* **More kinds of diagram**: state diagrams (an automaton's accepting states as double circles), sequence diagrams, timelines and mind maps, alongside the flowcharts.
* **Formula plots with sliders.** When a lecture gives a formula, your notes can draw it exactly: drag a slider to see what a parameter does, hover for readings, guess the shape before it's shown, and see heat maps and what a matrix does to the plane.
* **Labelled drawings.** A lecture about a thing (a drone, a hand, a circuit) can get a drawing whose parts you can point at, put together in seconds from a library of openly licensed parts. Something the library has no parts for is drawn from scratch in the background.
* **Your notes first, their diagrams a moment later.** Notes are filed and readable as soon as they're written. Diagrams, plots and drawings are added to the open note in place: the page doesn't jump, and changes you made to the note file are kept.
* **Rich notes switches.** Settings → AI engines → Rich notes turns diagrams, formula plots and drawings off together or one at a time. Off, you get plain notes and nothing extra is asked of your AI.
* **Claude Code speed.** Standard, Fast mode (the same Opus, up to 2.5 times faster) or a Quicker model, for writing your notes and designing their diagrams. Rich notes and speed are saved on your library, so you can change them from a laptop or the library's own web page.
* **The recorder's first words within a second or two.** Cactus Whistle, a 17 MB speech model that comes inside the app, shows what's said while the recorder or the menu bar dropdown is open. Your chosen model still writes the transcript your notes are made from, and its lines replace the quick ones as it catches up.
* **Transcribe after class.** Settings → Recording → When it's written down → After class only records during the lecture, which saves battery, and writes it down once you stop.
* **AI apps on one computer, no Tailscale.** Settings → AI tool access connects Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI in one click, and Disconnect puts their settings back the way they were.
* **A much quieter app.** Study Stash idles at under half a percent of a processor, and recording costs about a quarter of what it did.
* **Settings fixes.** Keep recordings now removes old audio within a minute of changing it. Renaming a class on the library's web page takes its lectures, folder and Canvas course with it, as the app does. Open when you log in shows how it really is. A laptop has its own Updates row with Check now, Update now and Update automatically.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Not signed yet.** The installers are ready to be signed but aren't yet. On a Mac, if macOS says it can't check Study Stash, click Done, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. On Windows, if you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
* **Fast mode needs usage credits** on your Claude account and is billed at a higher rate than your plan's own usage. Without credits, Claude Code just runs at its normal speed. It only speeds up Opus: if you picked Sonnet or Haiku for your notes, they stay as they are.
* **Cactus Whistle is for following along.** Its live words are rougher than the final transcript, and it reads English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch and Polish. A lecture in another language shows your chosen model's lines, about half a minute behind. You can also pick Whistle as your transcription model if you want the fastest and smallest; it misses more names and terms than the others.
* **Drawings need Claude Code, Codex or Gemini.** Ollama's local models draw flowcharts only.
* **Claude on the web and the phone app** still need Tailscale Funnel to reach your library. The apps on your computer don't.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording. To update now, use Settings → Your library → Library → Update now (a laptop: Settings → General → Updates).
