## What's new

* **Whiteboard photos.** While your computer records a lecture, Study Stash on your iPhone shows "Recording on your laptop" with a camera button. Photos you take go with that lecture and the moment you took them, with nothing to pick. When the notes are written, the AI looks at the photos and puts what's on the board into the notes where it was written: equations, lists, tables and diagrams. A drawing that doesn't copy well is shown as the photo itself. The lecture's page shows its photos in order, each with its time. Claude looks at the photos themselves; with other AIs, the words read from them are used.
* **Ask knows what's due.** Ask is now told today's date, your Canvas due list and today's and tomorrow's calendar with every question, so "what's due this Sunday?" gets the real answer, from your phone or your computer.
* **Your phone knows which AI is answering**, so it can show that AI while you wait for an answer.

## Fixes

* A photo sent from your phone while a lecture is still recording waits for that lecture and joins it, instead of being turned away.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Your phone:** whiteboard photos need the Study Stash iPhone app's next update too.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → General → Updates → Update now, or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
