## What's new

* **Keep recording a note.** A note you recorded on this computer has a Keep recording button beside its Notes and Transcript tabs. What's said now goes on the end of the same note's transcript, your own notes stay as you typed them, and once you stop, its notes are written again from all of it. It works days later too, after the old sound has been cleared away.
* **A recording is on Home from the moment it starts.** It shows as "New note" (or the title you typed), and where its summary will go it says where it is: recording, being written down, on its way to your library, or writing notes. Click it to open it at any point, no waiting for the notes.

## Fixes

* **Clicking the Record pill while it records opens the recording.** On Home and a folder's page the red pill used to stop the recording when clicked, so going back to it to take notes ended it. Now a click opens its page, and only the stop square in the pill stops it.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → Your library → Library → Update now (a laptop: Settings → General → Updates), or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
