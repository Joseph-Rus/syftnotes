## New

* **Egghead Notes is now Syft Notes**, with a new icon: a pixel s that sorts loose bits into clean rows. The app, the iPhone app, the Canvas extension and the website (now [syftnotes.com](https://syftnotes.com)) all say Syft Notes. Copies already installed (Study Stash or Egghead Notes) update to it by themselves and take the new name and icon in Finder, the Dock, the menu bar, the Start menu and notifications.
* **Setting up is one button.** A first run is one window with Get started: it makes your library on this computer, asks for the microphone, and you can record straight away, even while speech-to-text is still downloading (that lecture is written down as soon as it arrives).
* **Free study notes with your Google account.** The first lecture's page offers Continue with Google: sign in once with a personal Google account and notes are written after every class, free, through Gemini. A Claude or ChatGPT plan works as before, and copy and paste is there as another free way.
* **Installing is one click.** On Windows, Setup asks nothing and opens the app when it's done. On a Mac, the disk opens with Syft Notes beside Applications, and opening the app straight from the download offers to move it into Applications for you.
* **The new look, everywhere.** Loading, thinking and recording now use the pixel s: the recording dot is one square cell, the sound level is a column of cells, and the app opens with the s sorting itself into place.

## Fixes

* When an AI's allowance runs out (such as Google's free week), each lecture is still filed with its transcript, says when its notes will come, and gets them by itself once the AI answers again.
* The notification question waits for your first recording to finish, instead of appearing as soon as setup closes.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → General → Updates → Update now, or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
* **Windows taskbar:** an icon pinned to the taskbar takes the new picture, but keeps its old name when you hover over it until you unpin it and pin it again.
* **The Canvas extension** is version 1.7, with the new name and icon.
