## What's new

* **A new look.** Egghead Notes is now black, grey and white, with one easy-to-read typeface throughout and simple outline icons. Each class has its own highlighter colour, and red is kept for recording and for anything late. Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Ollama are shown by their own logos wherever the app names them.
* **One sidebar, one page.** The library window is a single sidebar (Home, Due, your folders, and Coming up) with one page beside it. Home shows today's date, what's most pressing, what's up next today and tomorrow (click one to record it), and every note, newest first. Notes that aren't in a folder wait on Home instead of in Unsorted.
* **Clubs and Work.** The sidebar keeps folders under Classes, Clubs and Work. A folder under Classes holds lectures; one anywhere else holds meetings. To move a note, click its folder under its title and pick another; Undo puts it back.
* **Take notes while you record.** Recording opens the note's page right away, with Pause and Stop across its top, and you can type your own notes as it records. Start a line with "# " for a heading or "- " for a bullet. When the notes are written, every line of yours is kept, in your order, and shown in ink, with what was added shown in grey.
* **A folder's page and Due's calendar.** A folder has one page: when it meets, Attach and Record, its notes by week, and what's due next. Due is now a month calendar, with each assignment in its class's colour, "Missing" in red when it's late, and the week as a planner.
* **Ask your AI can file clubs and jobs.** In Settings, Ask your AI can now add a club to Clubs or a job to Work, and move a class to another folder.

## Fixed

* Saving Settings no longer removes a class or folder that was added somewhere else while Settings was open, and saving the library's web Settings page keeps each class in its folder.
* Gemini (through Antigravity) can read the files it needs while it writes notes. Before, it sometimes stopped with an empty answer.
* If an AI finishes without writing anything, Egghead Notes now treats that as a failure instead of saving empty notes over a lecture's old ones.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → Your library → Library → Update now (a laptop: Settings → General → Updates), or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
