## Changes

* **One Add a recording button.** Record no longer has its own small menu that did the same thing as **Add a recording** beside it. Press **Add a recording** to bring in a recording you already have, or drop the file on Home or a class's page.
* **Windows: signed by its publisher.** Setup.exe and the app are now signed, so Windows shows who made them.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11; on Linux, a desktop with PipeWire or PulseAudio.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within about an hour, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → General → Updates → Update now.
