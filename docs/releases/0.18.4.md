## What's new

* **An anonymous daily count.** Once a day Study Stash sends one count so we know how many people use it: a random code made for this copy of the app, the day, the app's version, and whether it's a Mac or a PC. Nothing else: no name, no account, no email, nothing you record or write. Turn it off any time in **Settings → General → Count this copy**; turning it off also deletes the code. The privacy policy says exactly what's sent.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → General → Updates → Update now, or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
