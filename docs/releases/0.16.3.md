## What's new

* **Canvas in Firefox.** Study Stash for Canvas is now on Mozilla's add-ons site, so if Firefox is the browser you use (or Zen, LibreWolf or Waterfox), connecting Canvas sets it up there. **Add to Firefox** opens the add-on's page and shows a code. On that page click the blue **Add to Firefox** button, then **Add**. Click the add-on's Study Stash button (it may be under the puzzle-piece Extensions button), paste the code, click **Connect**, then **Allow**. Study Stash says when Firefox is connected and goes on to your courses. Until now it sent Firefox users to Chrome or Edge.
* **Safari and Firefox, no Chrome.** Safari can't run the extension. If Firefox is the only other browser on your computer, Study Stash now uses it, where before it told you to get Chrome.

## Good to know

* **Already reading Canvas in Chrome, Edge or another browser?** Nothing changes. Canvas keeps syncing there.
* **Firefox:** it needs Firefox 140 or later. The add-on is the same extension Chrome runs, with the same promise: it only reads, with your own Canvas sign-in, and what it reads goes only to your own library.
* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → Your library → Library → Update now (a laptop: Settings → General → Updates), or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
