## What's new

* **Claude stays signed in.** Claude Code renews its sign-in about every eight hours. When several of Egghead Notes' jobs (sorting, notes, diagrams, Ask) started at that moment, they could all try to renew it at once and knock the sign-in over, so Claude asked you to sign in again. Now, after a quiet spell, the first job signs in alone and the rest follow a few seconds later. A job Claude turns away is tried once more before Egghead Notes asks you to sign in. Egghead Notes never reads or keeps your Claude sign-in: it only decides when to start Claude.

## Fixes

* Your phone's **Due** tab works when it's connected through Google: it shows whether Canvas is linked and syncs when you ask.
* **Stop** on your phone stops the answer on your computer too, so a quick follow-up no longer says the chat is still answering.
* Chats can be renamed, and brought back after Delete, from your phone.
* Small buttons, dropdowns and the ask bar have an even, fine outline. In dark mode some showed a light rim along the top only.
* "Another Claude process is refreshing the sign-in" no longer counts as signed out: Egghead Notes waits it out.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Your phone:** the Due tab and Stop fixes reach your phone through this computer's update; the phone app itself doesn't need one.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → General → Updates → Update now, or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
