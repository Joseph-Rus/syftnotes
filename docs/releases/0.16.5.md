## Fixes

* **The level bars move with the voice.** The little bars beside the time while you record (on the Record pill, in the menu bar dropdown and on the floating recorder) were updated four times a second, so they jumped. They now follow the sound twenty times a second while they're on screen, and stop entirely when they aren't.
* **Spinners turn smoothly.** A loading spinner turned a step each time the app got round to it, so it crawled whenever the app was busy. It now turns at a steady speed whatever else is going on.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → Your library → Library → Update now (a laptop: Settings → General → Updates), or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
