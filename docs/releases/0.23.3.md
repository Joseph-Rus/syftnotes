## Fixes

* **On a Mac, opening the Syft Notes download works again.** Since 0.20.0, opening the downloaded Syft-Notes.dmg showed no window, and on recent macOS Finder could quit. The disk image is now made the standard way.
* **Two Syft Notes folders become one.** If your notes ended up in two Syft Notes folders (for example an old OneDrive and a school OneDrive), Syft Notes asks which one to keep and copies the other folder's notes into it. Nothing is deleted, and where two copies of a lecture differ, both are kept.
* **Signing in to Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini never moves notes off the AI on your computer.** If you set your notes or Ask to the free AI on this computer, signing in to another AI leaves that choice as it is.
* **The free AI on your computer picks a size that fits it,** and its problems are explained in plain words in Ask.
* **Canvas class pages keep your place** when Canvas syncs: an open week stays open, and a list that couldn't load doesn't empty the rest of the page.
* **Back and Forward return you to where you were scrolled.**
* **Quick search** starts at the top of each new set of results, and on Windows in dark mode the chosen result is easy to read.
* **Phone photos open without freezing the window.**
* **On Windows, Due dates are no longer cut off.**
* **On a Mac, the part that answers your phone with the app closed** waits for a moment if another copy is still finishing, instead of stopping.

## Good to know

* **If you downloaded Syft Notes for Mac before 0.23.3 and its window never appeared,** delete that download and download Syft Notes again: the new download opens properly. If the window still seems missing, it may have opened behind your browser.
* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11; on Linux, a desktop with PipeWire or PulseAudio.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within about an hour, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → General → Updates → Update now.
