## New

* **The Syft look.** Syft Notes now wears the colours of its pixel-s logo, light or dark: ink and paper, coral when you're recording, lilac for what's picked, sky for progress. The old teal is gone.
  * Buttons are the logo's square cells.
  * The recorder is the same on Mac and Windows.
  * The menu-bar dropdown (the tray on Windows) shows what's recording and your recent lectures.
  * Instead of twenty colour themes there's light, dark or follow your computer. A theme you picked before still loads, in the new look.
* **Smaller updates that never ask for a password.** Syft Notes now installs and updates a new way, so each update brings only what changed. You don't need to do anything: your next update moves you over by itself, and your lectures, settings and AI apps stay as they are.
  * **On a Mac,** the app is now called **Syft Notes** in your Applications folder (it was "Study Stash"). It renames itself the first time it opens.
  * **On Windows,** the first time Syft Notes is idle after this update, it moves to its new home and opens again within about a minute. Your Start menu entry, taskbar pin and start at login come with it.
  * **On Linux,** the download is now an AppImage.
* **Gemini sign-in works with Google's code.** Signing in to Gemini ends on a Google page with a code to paste. Syft Notes now has a box for it, right where you signed in.
* **One "Are you sure?"** Every question, like deleting a lecture, now opens over the window you're in. The safe button comes first, and Escape always answers no.

## Fixes

* **"The microphone didn't start"** no longer shows up with a working microphone while Syft Notes is busy, for example just after it opens.
* **Listen** no longer closes the app when a lecture plays to its end.
* **On Windows, an AI's change to a lecture can always be undone.** Sometimes it wasn't recorded, so Undo couldn't bring the lecture back.
* **A follow-up question** asked the moment an answer finishes is no longer refused as "still answering".
* **Phone photos go with the right lecture** when you keep recording, or a planned lecture starts.
* **Home no longer says "No notes yet"** when Syft Notes couldn't reach your notes. It says what went wrong.
* **A page that takes too long** says so, instead of quietly showing nothing.
* **"Rewriting…"** no longer gets stuck when the library drops out for a moment.
* **After moving a note, pressing Undo, and moving it again,** the "Moved · Undo" message stays for its full time.
* **Progress bars** are a deeper blue, so you can see them at a glance.

## Good to know

* **On Linux,** some systems need FUSE before an AppImage will run: install `libfuse2` (or `libfuse2t64` on Ubuntu 24.04). Until then, your current copy keeps working and says what to install.
* **On Windows,** the installer still shows "unknown publisher" for now.
* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11; on Linux, a desktop with PipeWire or PulseAudio.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → General → Updates → Update now.
