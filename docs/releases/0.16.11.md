## Fixes

This release has no new features. Every screen of the app was gone through by hand, button by button, and these are the things that turned up.

**Your notes**

* **Rewriting notes can't lose them.** Rewrite on a lecture replaced its notes even when the AI wrote nothing: it wasn't installed or signed in, its limit was reached, or notes were switched off. The notes, the title and the topics were gone for good. A lecture now keeps everything it has unless new notes were really written, and its page says why the rewrite didn't happen.
* **What you type stays on the page.** Lines typed while recording disappeared from the note's page once the library had filed it, whenever no notes were written from the recording (a short one, or with no AI). They show now, as "What you wrote", and Edit opens them.
* **A recording too short for notes says so.** Notes are written from about a minute and a half of speech. A shorter recording was promised notes and then showed an empty page. Its page, the notice when it stops and its note file now say it's too short, where its transcript is, and that Keep recording adds to it.
* **With notes switched off, nothing promises notes.** After setup's "No AI for now", Home, the recording's page and the notices still said notes were on their way. They now say notes are off and where to turn them on.
* **Keep recording keeps the note's name.** Recording more into a finished note put its working title back and then gave it a new name. It keeps the name it has, and a name you typed always stays.
* **A recording stopped in under 5 seconds says it wasn't kept**, and Keep recording stopped that soon says the note was left as it was. A recording made in a class also stays in that class on its page while it's being filed (it read "Unsorted" for a few seconds).

**Asking and searching**

* **A question about a recording in progress is answered from that recording.** With the scope on this lecture, it also searched every other lecture and could cite moments that weren't in it.
* **Cited moments open where they were said.** The times under an answer, and a search result that comes from a transcript, now open the transcript at that line with the line marked. They opened the notes at the top.

**The library window**

* **A deleted lecture leaves Home at once, and Undo stays.** It stayed in Home's list until the page was read again, and Undo went after 10 seconds though the question promised a few minutes. Undo now stays five minutes, with a × to put it away.
* **Due's week shows which class meetings were recorded.**
* **Home's "Enjoying Egghead Notes?" card shows whole.** Since 0.16.0 it was cut off under its first line, with its three buttons hidden.
* **Settings → Canvas says which course each class is linked to.** The box was empty for a course that Find my courses hadn't listed.

**Setting up**

* **A library password with an accented letter, another alphabet or an emoji is refused as you type it,** with what to use instead. Such a password could never be sent, so setup waited 30 seconds, said the library didn't start, and failed every way on after that, even after quitting.
* **Setup's smaller snags:** a class typed but not added is added by Continue; "Open when you log in" shows what you last chose and no longer turns itself on; the Notes step opens on what's set now; clicking Claude's or ChatGPT's row does what its button does; an empty school address says what to type; the list of transcription models can be cancelled; and messages under the fields show their whole sentence.

**Phone and tablet**

* **A phone pairs with the code typed the way Settings shows it.** "123 456" with its space lost its last digit.
* **On an iPad held upright, a lecture's notes fit the screen and scroll.** A wide table scrolls sideways in a box of its own.

**Screen readers**

* **VoiceOver and Narrator say what every button says.** Buttons made of an icon and a label, switches and list rows were read as "Avalonia.Controls.Grid" and the like: the sidebar's rows, Only my notes, and six to thirteen controls on every Settings page.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → Your library → Library → Update now (a laptop: Settings → General → Updates), or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
