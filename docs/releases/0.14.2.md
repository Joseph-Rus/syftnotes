## What's new

* **The recording pill wears your theme.** While a lecture records, the small pill on your screen is now the colour of the theme you picked in Settings → Appearance, in light and dark, and it's a little smaller. Before, it was black whatever the theme.
* **Buy us a coffee.** Egghead Notes is free, and two students build it. If it helps you, the new button on Home, beside Record, opens our Ko-fi page. It replaces the one that was in Settings → About.

## Fixes

* **The recorder follows your pointer.** Dragged near an edge of the screen, or out of the corner it starts in, the recorder lagged behind the pointer, and on a Mac its frosted background trailed after it. It now moves with the pointer wherever you take it.
* **A lecture's notes header lines up.** On a Mac, "Written by Claude · Mon 8:36" sat higher than **Summary**, and Edit and Rewrite notes sat lower. They're on one line now.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → Your library → Library → Update now (a laptop: Settings → General → Updates), or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
