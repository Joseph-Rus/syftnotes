## What's new

* **Notes come quicker after class when speakers are told apart.** With Settings → Recording → Tell speakers apart in the transcript on, the voices are now told apart ten minutes at a time in the background while the lecture records. Once you stop, only the last stretch is left, so a lecture's notes start about half a minute after it ends rather than several minutes later (a 68-minute lecture: 30 seconds, not 3¾ minutes). Each voice is recognised across the pieces by how it sounds. With the setting off, nothing changes: the notes start as soon as the transcript is done.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → Your library → Library → Update now (a laptop: Settings → General → Updates), or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
