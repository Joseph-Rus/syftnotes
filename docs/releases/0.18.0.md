## What's new

* **Your phone, through your Google account.** If Egghead Notes is kept on just this computer, Settings → Phone → **Connect with Google** links your phone with no VPN and nothing to install on the computer. Pick your Google account and press Allow. Then open Egghead Notes on your iPhone or iPad (the iPhone app is in testing now), tap Continue with Google with the same account, or scan the code Settings shows. Your notes go into a private Egghead Notes folder in your Google Drive that only Egghead Notes can see. Your phone keeps a copy, so it reads your lectures even while your computer is off. Recordings, typed notes, notebook pages, files, voice memos and questions from your phone reach your computer when it's on. Ask from the phone takes a few seconds longer than on the computer. An Android phone or the phone's web page can still connect over Tailscale, under Other ways.
* **Adding a phone over Tailscale takes one scan.** The QR code now carries the code, so the phone is added as soon as it scans. The six digits stay on screen in case you need to type them.
* **Gemini sets up like Claude and ChatGPT.** Pick Gemini in setup and Egghead Notes gets it ready, signs it in, and finishes the setup chat on Gemini. Signed out, Gemini has a Sign in button on the notes step and in Settings → Your AI. Open in Antigravity opens it signed in, with your lectures.

## Fixes

* Where Google doesn't offer Gemini, setup says so in plain words and lets you pick another AI, instead of waiting.
* Egghead Notes' Gemini setup never touches your own Gemini settings folder.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Google accounts:** a personal Google account works. Some schools don't let their Google accounts connect to outside apps; Egghead Notes says so if yours doesn't, and a personal Gmail works instead.
* **A library on another computer:** phones still connect to it over Tailscale, as before.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → General → Updates → Update now, or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
