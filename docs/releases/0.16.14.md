## What's new

* **Add the Canvas extension from the Chrome Web Store.** Egghead Notes for Canvas is on the Chrome Web Store now, so in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc and Vivaldi the Canvas step opens its page there: click Add to Chrome (Get extension in Edge), paste the code Egghead Notes shows, and click Allow. No more loading a folder in Developer mode. Opera keeps the folder, and if your school's Chrome won't install from the store, "Load it from a folder instead" is right there.
* **Egghead Notes tells you when Claude needs you to sign in again.** When Claude's sign-in runs out, a lecture's notes can't be written. The lecture now says so ("Your notes weren't written because Claude needs you to sign in again"), in the app, on your phone and on your library's pages. The notification for that lecture has a Sign in button, and Settings → Your AI shows a card to sign in. After you sign in, its Sign in button becomes Check: press it, and Egghead Notes writes the notes it missed by itself.

## Good to know

* **Already have the extension from a folder?** It keeps working. You don't need to change anything.
* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → Your library → Library → Update now (a laptop: Settings → General → Updates), or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
