## Fixes

* **On a Mac, the Syft Notes icon in your Dock works again.** If you had kept Study Stash in your Dock, its icon showed a question mark after 0.23.0 renamed the app to Syft Notes, and clicking it did nothing. The first time this version opens, it points that icon at Syft Notes for you. Your Dock blinks once, and the icon stays where you put it. You don't need to do anything.
* **New versions arrive sooner.** Syft Notes now looks for a new version 2 minutes after it opens and then every hour (it was every 6 hours), so a fix reaches you the same day.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11; on Linux, a desktop with PipeWire or PulseAudio.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within about an hour, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → General → Updates → Update now.
