## What's new

* **Setup looks like the rest of Study Stash.** Setup has the app's own look now: its steps down the side like the library's sidebar, each page in the same panel as Home, bigger titles, and the app's own buttons. Continue is the one dark button on each page, Back sits beside it, and "Skip for now" and "Set up by hand" are proper links. Nothing about what setup asks or does has changed.

## Fixes

* **Recording no longer holds on to pages you've left.** Every page you pressed Record on, and every recording's own page, stayed in memory until you quit Study Stash. They're let go as soon as you leave them now.
* **The recording button no longer collects old names.** While recording, the sidebar's recording button told screen readers the time every second and kept every one of those names, about 1 MB for each hour you recorded, until you quit. It keeps only the newest now.
* **"Change shortcuts" on setup's last page reads properly to a screen reader.** It was read out as "Avalonia.Controls.StackPanel".

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → Your library → Library → Update now (a laptop: Settings → General → Updates), or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
