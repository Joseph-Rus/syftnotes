## What's new

* **A smaller recorder pill on a Mac.** While a lecture records, the pill floating over your other apps is now a small upright capsule: the Egghead Notes S over three level bars, with no class name or timer. Point at it and the S turns into Stop, which stops with one click (Resume while paused). A card beside it shows the class, how long it's been recording and the last two lines heard. Clicking anywhere else on the pill opens the recorder, as before, and you can still drag it anywhere.
* **The pill shows over full-screen apps.** Full-screen slides, a full-screen Zoom call or a full-screen browser no longer hide it. The app keeps its Dock icon while it does.

## Fixes

* **The pill shows up again.** 0.16.7's attempt to float it over full-screen apps kept it from showing at all for some, and could take the library window off the screen when you switched to another app. That's undone, replaced by the change above, and opening the library now always puts the pill away.
* **Updates wait for a lecture's notes.** An update could start the moment a lecture was sent to the library on the same computer, restarting the library while it wrote the notes. One quit stuck partway for two hours, and the notes waited all that time. Updates now wait until notes from a recent lecture are written, and a quit that takes longer than 30 seconds ends anyway, so the new version always opens.
* **A deleted recording stays deleted.** Deleting a lecture while its notes were still being written could bring it back in Unsorted a few seconds later.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → Your library → Library → Update now (a laptop: Settings → General → Updates), or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
