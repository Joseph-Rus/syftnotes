## Fixes

* **On a Mac, Syft Notes opens again.** With "Answer your phone when Syft Notes is closed" on, opening the app from Spotlight, Finder or the Dock could say *The application "Syft Notes" is not open anymore.* and nothing would open. The part that keeps answering your phone in the background was being taken for the app itself. It isn't any more.
* **Nothing fails silently.** A page that couldn't load used to stay blank or out of date. Now it says something went wrong, so you know to try again.
* **Your AI's status agrees everywhere.** The AI rows in Settings and the cards about a problem with your AI now read the same thing, so one can't say "Not set up" while the other says you're connected.
* **Text that's cut short can be read.** Hover over a name, title or message that ends in "…" to see all of it.
* **The AI pickers are easier to read.** The picked AI's line and the ones you can't pick yet now have clear contrast.
* **On a Mac, the Get-ready window stays in the Dock** while it's open, and has the usual Window menu (⌘W, ⌘M) and ⌘, for Settings.
* **Screen readers** now name the buttons that only had an icon: remove, search, close and dismiss.

## Good to know

* **If Syft Notes already won't open on your Mac:** this update can't reach a Mac that's stuck, so do one of these once:
  * restart your Mac (or log out and back in), then open Syft Notes; or
  * open Activity Monitor, quit the "Syft Notes" that has no window, then open Syft Notes within half a minute.
* **The Canvas extension:** the Chrome Web Store and Firefox Add-ons both have version 1.8 now. Setup finds your school's Canvas by itself in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, Vivaldi and Firefox, with nothing to type.
* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11; on Linux, a desktop with PipeWire or PulseAudio.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → General → Updates → Update now.
