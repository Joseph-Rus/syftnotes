## Fixes

* **On a Mac, moving Syft Notes into Applications works properly.** A copy moved there from the download kept a "downloaded from the internet" mark, so it asked to move again every time it opened and couldn't update itself. It no longer does, and a copy that already has the mark is healed the next time it moves. Moving never replaces a newer copy with an older one, and the download disk is ejected afterwards.
* **An older copy of Syft Notes kept somewhere else no longer starts when you log in.** A copy left in Downloads, a second Applications folder or a "Syft Notes 2" now hands everything (start at login, the background library, your AI apps' connections) to the newest copy.
* **Updates arrive even if your laptop sleeps a lot.** Syft Notes looks for a new version by the clock, not by how long the laptop has been awake.
* **A lecture that's waiting to be written down no longer holds updates back.** If the speech model was missing, updates could wait forever.
* **No update or move while setup is open,** so setup is never restarted in the middle.
* **On Windows, "Open Syft Notes when you log in" stays on after you reinstall.**
* **On Windows, opening an older installer when Syft Notes is already installed opens your copy** instead of making a second one.
* **Setup's step counter** counts correctly when your school doesn't use Canvas.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11; on Linux, a desktop with PipeWire or PulseAudio.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within about an hour, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → General → Updates → Update now.
