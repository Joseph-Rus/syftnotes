## What's new

* **Select and copy your notes.** Press and drag across a lecture's notes to select them, as on a web page. A selection can run from one paragraph into the next, through bullets, tables and formulas. Copy with ⌘C (Ctrl+C on Windows) or right-click and choose Copy; you don't need Share any more. A double click selects a word, a triple click a paragraph, and Shift and a click stretch the selection. ⌘A (Ctrl+A) selects all of the notes. Dragging past the top or bottom of the page scrolls it.
* **What you copy keeps its shape.** Each bullet keeps its "• " (a numbered item its number), a table's cells are a tab apart, so they paste into a spreadsheet, a definition reads "Term: meaning", and a formula comes out as LaTeX, the text form of a formula.
* **Ask's answers select the same way.** In Ask and the quick panel, a selection runs across a whole answer now, not just one paragraph.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → General → Updates → Update now, or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
