## What's new

* **Settings you can find your way around.** The sidebar is in four groups, most used first: Egghead Notes (General, Appearance, Recording, Calendars, Phone), Your library (Classes, Notes, Your AI, Canvas), Connect and import (AI apps, Granola, Your files) and Advanced (Library, Connection). General now has the version and updates in one place (your library's too, when it's this computer), the keyboard shortcuts, and what you use Egghead Notes for. The doubled rows are gone: "Use just this computer" and "Start the library when this Mac starts" are each in one place now, and the credits fold away under About.
* **Setup explains itself.** Its first page says how Egghead Notes works before asking anything: you record, the notes are written for you, everything's filed and searchable, and it's on your phone too. The last page says what to do next.
* **Not just for school.** Setup asks what you'll use Egghead Notes for: school, work, research, creative work or personal. Without school there's no Canvas step, classes are called folders, and notes are written for meetings and talks (a summary, decisions, action items with who and when, open questions) instead of study notes. Change it any time in Settings → General.
* **Recurring events find their class.** When an event on your calendar looks like one of your classes ("Machine & Deep Learning" for Machine Learning), Coming up offers to file every one of them there. Once you say yes, every week's event, and what you record in it, goes to that class.
* **Ready for the iPhone and iPad app.** The library takes lectures recorded and written down on a phone, and Settings → Phone says how to add one with the app. Phones on Android keep the web app, which can now make flashcards and a quiz from any lecture.

## Fixes

* **Links in Settings point where things are now.** "Under Your library → Library" and similar lines name the new places.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → General → Updates → Update now, or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
