## What's new

* **Pages sit in the middle of the window.** A lecture's page is one column, as wide as its notes, centred in the window with its Ask bar under it. Home, Due and a folder's page stop at a comfortable width and centre too. Nothing hugs the left edge when the sidebar is shut or the window is wide.
* **Record an event from the menu bar.** Each event under Coming up in the menu bar dropdown (or the tray panel on Windows) is now a button: click one to record it, as in the library window.

## Fixes

* **The class picker in the menu bar dropdown opens.** The button with two arrows beside Record chooses the class a recording is filed in. On a Mac its list opened behind the dropdown, so the button seemed to do nothing. The list now opens in the dropdown, under Record.
* The line "The library sorts it by what's said" under Record is gone.

## Good to know

* **Needs:** on a Mac, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, Apple silicon or Intel; on Windows, Windows 10 or 11.
* **Updating:** installed copies update themselves within a few hours, when nothing is recording and you aren't typing in a lecture's notes. To update now, use Settings → Your library → Library → Update now (a laptop: Settings → General → Updates), or Update now from the menu bar on a Mac.
